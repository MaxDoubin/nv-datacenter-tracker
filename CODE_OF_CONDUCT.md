# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue about evidence, not people.

## Specifics

This project documents public spending by named companies and public bodies. That invites strong opinions
about data centers, taxes and economic development. Those opinions are welcome in the world; this repository
is for the record.

**Expected:**

- Bring documents. A claim with a source beats a claim with conviction.
- Assume error before bad faith. Every material discrepancy catalogued here looks like a spreadsheet mistake,
  and is described that way.
- Accept correction. Being wrong in public and fixing it is the point of an open dataset.

**Not accepted:**

- Personal attacks, harassment, or abuse — toward contributors, public officials, or employees of any company
  in the data.
- Editing figures to support a conclusion. Changing a number without a source, or removing an inconvenient
  one, is the only thing here that gets an immediate ban.
- Using the repository to allege wrongdoing by a named individual. Document what the records say and let
  readers draw conclusions.

## Enforcement

Reports go to the repository owner via a private issue or GitHub's report function. Consequences range from a
warning to a permanent ban, depending on severity. Enforcement decisions are the maintainer's and are final.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), shortened to the
parts that actually apply here.
